# Minimal SIDE chest-fabric prompt library

Use these formulations for an adult `CHARACTER_BASE` strict SIDE view when an extreme-micro upper panel expands into a conventional side cup. They are a sequential fallback chain, not phrases to concatenate. Preserve the authoritative body source and its complete outer silhouette in every attempt.

## 1. QUADRANT_LATERAL_SECTOR

Preferred default. User-approved calibration result; passed `1/1`.

```text
Use anatomical sectors only as a placement coordinate system for the fully opaque upper garment panel. Confine the compact panel to the medial sector toward the sternum and intermammary line. Equivalently, the opaque panel occupies the upper-inner and lower-inner quadrants. The lateral sector toward the armpit contains no teal fabric. The panel's lateral boundary follows approximately the 12-to-6 meridian. The central coordinate landmark remains securely inside the opaque panel.

This is a minimal extreme-micro SIDE topology, not a conventional bikini cup: only a narrow lower-contour edge is visible in strict profile, with no broad side panel and no fabric extending under the armpit. Perspective hides most of the inward-positioned panel. Preserve the body's exact external chest projection and complete silhouette; do not flatten, shrink, lift, squeeze, separate, or reshape anatomy.
```

Hard visual gate: reject if a triangular/cup-shaped panel remains visible on the outer lateral surface or extends toward the armpit.

Evaluate fabric and body independently. A user may accept the panel size/placement as the lower fabric boundary while rejecting the same image because chest projection shrank. In that case, freeze the accepted fabric extent for the next attempt, mark the image `BODY_SILHOUETTE_FAIL`, and restore chest depth from the authoritative FRONT/SIDE body source. Do not keep reducing fabric and do not promote the anatomy-failed frame as body authority.

## 2. MEDIAL_SURFACE

Use alone only after repeated failure of `QUADRANT_LATERAL_SECTOR`. Passed `1/1`; slightly broader than the preferred result.

```text
Apply MEDIAL_SURFACE alone. Place the compact fully opaque upper panel on the medial surface toward the sternum. Keep the lateral surface outside the panel and wrap the material inward. In strict profile, show only the panel's narrow lower rim. No broad fabric panel may appear on the outer side surface or below the armpit. Preserve the body's complete external chest contour and projection exactly; the garment must not reshape anatomy.
```

## 3. MEDIAL_CRESCENT

Third fallback only. Passed `1/1`; slightly more forward coverage.

```text
Shape the compact fully opaque upper panel as a medial crescent centered toward the sternum. Its outer boundary follows the 12-to-6 meridian. The crescent includes the central coordinate landmark and does not spread onto the camera-facing lateral side. Perspective turns most of it away from view, leaving a clean narrow lower-contour strip instead of a full cup. Preserve the body's complete external chest contour and projection exactly.
```

## Required exclusions

Use these outcome constraints with each single formulation:

```text
No conventional triangle cup, no broad lateral side panel, no fabric under the armpit, no sports-bra expansion, no lifted or flattened chest, no changed chest projection, and no anatomy or silhouette drift.
```

Do not use the already blocked/degraded formulations: `entire lateral half clear`, clock semicircle `12-3-6`, `outside garment` quadrant wording, radial sectors, strong-foreshortening front patches, or a sternum boundary combined with both outer quadrants explicitly empty.

If a fresh generation still expands the panel, perform one isolated same-stage edit that changes only colored garment pixels, uses `SIDE_LINE_ONLY_WORKING.png` as hard `CLOTHING_TOPOLOGY`, and pixel-locks the body's external contour. Reject the edit if chest projection or any other anatomy changes.
